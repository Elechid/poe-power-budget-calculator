PoE 供電預算計算器

120 W PoE 交換器夠唔夠供電畀兩組 CCTV 鏡頭？

按以下例子，總供電足夠，還要有至少六個可用 PoE 口，而且每口都能供應對應鏡頭的最大耗電。

例子 4 部 8 W 加 2 部 18 W，合計 68 W。加 20% 預留後需要 81.6 W，120 W 交換器剩 38.4 W。15 W 單口仍不足以應付 18 W 鏡頭的額定最大耗電。

適用限制 按原廠最大耗電計算，適用時包括紅外線、加熱器或 PTZ。另要核對 PoE 標準、線材損耗和其他裝置的耗電。

Is a 120 W PoE switch enough for mixed CCTV cameras?

It is enough for the example below if at least six PoE ports are free and each port meets its camera’s maximum draw.

Worked example Four 8 W cameras plus two 18 W cameras draw 68 W. With 20% reserve, budget 81.6 W, leaving 38.4 W on a 120 W switch. A 15 W port still cannot supply an 18 W camera at its rated maximum.

Limits Use the manufacturer’s maximum power draw with infrared, heating or PTZ where applicable. Check PoE standards and account for cable losses and other devices.
